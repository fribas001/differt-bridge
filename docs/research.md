# Research, notebooks and preserved evidence

Start with the [research notebooks](../notebooks/README.md) for runnable analysis and model images. Their
small data bundle supplements the original DiffeRT archive with vegetation and RIS
controls. The notebooks run offline after dependencies are installed. They do not
rerun historical scientific campaigns or install solver patches.

## Project archive

The [project-2026-11-11 release](https://github.com/fribas001/differt-bridge/releases/tag/project-2026-11-11)
provides source, historical results, scenes, runtime recovery assets and nineteen
comparison videos. Large archives are split into transport parts. Use its documented
`fetch.py` and `assets.json` workflow rather than treating a part as a complete ZIP.

- [Recovery instructions and video gallery](https://github.com/fribas001/differt-bridge/blob/research/dielectric-diffraction/docs/project-archive.md)
- [Publication and checksum receipt](https://github.com/fribas001/differt-bridge/blob/research/dielectric-diffraction/validation/project-publication.json)
- [Fresh replay limitations](https://github.com/fribas001/differt-bridge/blob/research/dielectric-diffraction/docs/reproducibility.md)

Restoration/import/dispatch checks preserve access to evidence; they do not prove
complete recomputation on another machine. Earlier studies still need full input
inventories and fresh numerical receipts. The notebook data manifest covers the
numerical replay inputs; a separate [visual manifest](../notebooks/assets/README.md)
covers model images and selected synthetic geometry. Neither covers entire historical campaigns.
